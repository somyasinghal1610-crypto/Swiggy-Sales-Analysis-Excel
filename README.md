# Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard — Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed in Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard transforms raw Swiggy order data into meaningful business insights related to sales performance, customer ratings, order volume, food type, geographical performance, and sales trends.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **data analysis, Excel dashboard development, data visualization, and business intelligence**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall Swiggy sales performance
- Track total sales and total orders
- Monitor average customer ratings
- Analyze average order value
- Compare Veg and Non-Veg sales
- Identify top-performing states and cities
- Analyze monthly, daily, and weekly sales trends
- Compare sales, ratings, and orders across quarters
- Provide interactive filtering for deeper analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Total sales generated are approximately **₹53.01M**.
- The average customer rating is **4.34**, indicating strong customer satisfaction.
- Non-Veg orders contribute a larger share of sales compared with Veg orders.
- **Bengaluru** is the highest-performing city among the top cities, generating approximately **₹5.46M** in sales.
- Saturday records the highest daily sales among the displayed weekdays.
- Q2 records the highest sales among the three quarters shown.
- Sales trends remain relatively consistent across the weekly period, with some noticeable fluctuations.
